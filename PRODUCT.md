# Blue Homes Interiors

register: brand

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for a Chennai home-interiors studio. One job: convince a homeowner
to book a free consultation (phone, WhatsApp, or form). Every page funnels there.

## Users
Chennai homeowners, 28-45, buying interiors for a first or second flat (2BHK/3BHK,
some 4BHK and commercial). They compare 3-4 studios, browse on mobile at night,
and decide on trust: proof of delivered work, transparent pricing, on-time delivery.
Not luxury buyers; value-conscious but quality-driven.

## Brand
- Name: Blue Homes Interiors. Velachery, Chennai. Est. 2019.
- Proof points: 700+ homes delivered, 4.9 Google rating, 280+ reviews,
  100% on-time delivery, full 3D preview before work begins, in-house team.
- Contact: +91 96770 67989 · contact@bluehomesinteriors.in · Mon-Sun 8:30-23:30.
- Voice: calm, precise, warm. Factory precision meets a livable home.
  No exclamation marks, no hype words, no em dashes.

## Strategic principles
- Photography carries the site. Interiors are a visual purchase.
- "Spec-sheet" grammar (index numbers, hairlines, label/value rows) expresses
  the factory-made precision positioning. It is the named section system.
- Dark ink sections for rhythm and gravity; paper-white carries the bulk.
- One CTA per fold. Phone number is always one tap away.

## Anti-references
- Generic Indian builder sites: gradient band heroes, Poppins 800, taxi-yellow buttons.
- SaaS template grammar: icon-card grids, checkmark lists, repeated uppercase kickers.
- Luxury-beige interior cliche: Playfair/Cormorant serifs, cream everything.
