We started with a simple observation: beautiful spaces shouldn't be difficult to live in, and functional spaces shouldn't be boring. At River Interior Design, we believe that the true measure of a successful project is not just how it looks on the day of handover, but how it feels five years later.

Based in Coimbatore, we've grown from a small design studio into a comprehensive turnkey interior firm. But despite our growth, our core methodology remains unchanged. We don't just decorate rooms; we design experiences. Whether it's a cozy residential home, a bustling restaurant, or a high-performance commercial office, we approach every square foot with intention.

The metaphor of a river is central to everything we do. A river is deep, calm, and always moving forward. It carves through solid stone with patience and persistence. That is how we approach design challenges. We navigate constraints smoothly, flowing around obstacles to create spaces that feel natural and inevitable.

Our team works closely with skilled artisans, trusted vendors, and precision manufacturers to ensure that the vision we present in our 3D renders is exactly what is delivered on site. We take the stress out of interior design, allowing our clients to enjoy the creative process while we handle the complex execution.

MEET THE FOUNDER

"A space is only successful if it improves the life of the person in it."

With over a decade of experience in the design industry, Divya founded River Interior Design to bridge the gap between high-end aesthetics and practical livability.

Her signature style blends warm minimalism with rich, tactile materials—creating spaces that feel sophisticated yet deeply comfortable. As the creative force behind every project, she ensures that the studio's high standards are consistently met, from the initial sketch to the final styling.

Every project is personal.

Divya personally leads every engagement from first consultation to final handover.
