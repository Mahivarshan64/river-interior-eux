07How It Works

Five steps. No surprises.

01 Free site visit We measure your home, hear how you live in it, and note what the building allows. No charge, no obligation.
02 3D design & fixed quote You walk through your future home in 3D and revise it until it is right. The quote you approve is the amount you pay.
03 Factory production Panels are machine-cut and edge-banded at the factory while your site is prepped. Cleaner, faster, more accurate than carpentry on site.
04 Installation Our own installation crew assembles everything to the drawing. A project manager updates you with photos at every milestone.
05 Handover & warranty A joint walkthrough against the approved render, then keys and warranty documents. We return for a free check at six months.
